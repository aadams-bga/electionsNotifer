About Illinois Answers Project

The Illinois Answers Project investigates significant problems in Chicago and Illinois through in-depth reporting and offers the most comprehensive solutions-focused reporting in the state. It's published by the Better Government Association, a nonprofit news organization founded in 1923 and winner of the 2022 Pulitzer Prize for local reporting.

Find more of our reporting and learn more about how to support our work at the Illinois Answers Project website. For questions about this project specifically, contact Andrew Adams at aadams@bettergov.org.

This project is aimed at helping reporters, activists and others interested in local government to follow the Chicago Board of Education campaigns. It is especially meant to support a city-wide reporting collaborative led by Chalkbeat Chicago and made possible by a grant from Press Forward, an initiative from the Chicago Community Trust.
